Recurring Entries

Each rule repeats every so many weeks, months or years, starting on its first date. Monthly and yearly rules keep that day of the month (or the last day of the month if that day does not exist). Entries appear as normal expenses or income with a “[Recurring]” note when you open the app on or after their date; a first date in the past catches up the entries since then.
